# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, and to behave in a respectful and constructive way.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing the private information of others without permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers at laurent.martin.aspera@fr.ibm.com.
All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and can temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they consider inappropriate.

The [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant apply.
